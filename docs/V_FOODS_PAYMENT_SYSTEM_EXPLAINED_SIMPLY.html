Where Does Payment Start?

Everything starts when a student or customer fills their cart on the V Foods app and taps "Pay via Paytm".

  • The V Foods app creates a temporary reservation for the items.
  • Our backend asks Paytm: "Please open a secure payment session for ₹250."
  • Paytm opens its secure screen for UPI, Cards, or Netbanking.

Safety Note: V Foods never sees or stores card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank passwords. Paytm handles the banking securely.

How Does the Order Become Confirmed?

We do not trust the customer's phone to tell us if payment succeeded (because phones can crash or lose signal).

  • Instead, Paytm’s official server directly contacts V Foods’ server via a private digital message (called a Webhook).
  • Our server verifies that the money actually arrived.
  • Our server checks that food is in stock, sets the order to CONFIRMED, and alerts the kitchen screen.

The Core Rule for Investors & Store Owners

✅ Guarantee 1: The Outlet Never Loses Food
The kitchen screen will never show an order unless the money is 100% verified in Paytm. No free food, no fake screenshots, no scam orders.
✅ Guarantee 2: The Customer Never Loses Money
If money is debited from the customer's bank but food cannot be made (e.g. sold out or shop closed), the system automatically refunds 100% of the money.

The Big Question: Money Debited, Order Not Confirmed?

What happened behind the scenes, why no one needs to panic, and how it auto-resolves

Why Does This Happen in Real Life?

On busy university campuses, mobile data often fluctuates right after a student types their UPI PIN.

1. Bank Debited: Bank takes ₹120 and gives it to Paytm.
2. Internet Drop: Student's phone loses signal or closes app.
3. Result: Paytm has the money, but V Foods didn't hear back yet.

Outcome A: The Order Gets Confirmed

If the student reconnects within 1–2 minutes, our server confirms with Paytm that payment succeeded. As long as the food is still available, the order instantly flips to Confirmed and the kitchen prepares the meal.

Outcome B: Automated 100% Refund

If the student waited too long (>10 minutes) or the item sold out before signal returned, V Foods never keeps the money. Our system immediately calls Paytm's Refund API and returns 100% to the student's UPI.

Bottom Line for Customers: You do not need to panic, scream at the counter, or make 10 phone calls. The system handles it automatically in under 2 minutes.

Rush Hour Stock Race & Refunds Explained

What happens when two students pay for the last item at the exact same second?

The 1:00 PM Lunch Rush: 2 Students, 1 Sandwich Remaining

Imagine User A and User B both tap "Pay via Paytm" for the last Paneer Sandwich at 1:00:00 PM. Both of their bank accounts debit ₹80 successfully. What happens?

User A (Arrives 0.05 seconds first):
1. System locks the inventory row.
2. Sees 1 sandwich left. Takes it.
3. Sandwich count becomes 0.
4. Order Confirmed • Token #42 Issued!
User B (Arrives 0.05 seconds later):
1. System checks inventory.
2. Sees 0 sandwiches left (out of stock).
3. Rejects the order.
4. Instantly triggers 100% Refund of ₹80!

What If the V Foods Server Crashes or Restarts?

Even if our server restarts in the middle of a transaction, nothing is lost:

  • Database Safety: Database transactions are atomic—they either finish 100% or cleanly roll back. No half-finished states.
  • Paytm Retries: Paytm will automatically re-send confirmation webhooks every few minutes until our server answers.
  • Background Reconciler: A background cron job scans for any order that has been pending for over 2 minutes and resolves it immediately.
