1. What is V FOODS?

V FOODS is a high-speed campus pre-ordering, prepaid digital wallet, and kitchen management system built for collegiate food courts and large festival stalls. It eliminates physical counter lines by separating payment from ordering.

⚡ Pre-Order Before Arriving

Students browse open campus canteens, select dishes, and order hot meals before walking over.

💰 Zero-Latency Digital Wallet

Checkout completes atomically in under 20ms from a pre-loaded balance, with 0% gateway dropouts.

🍳 Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Kitchen cooks receive pre-orders instantly on a live digital Kanban board, preparing plates in advance.

🎟️ 4-Digit Token Pickup

Students show a 4-digit token or digital QR code at the counter, pick up hot food in seconds, and leave.

3. The 4-Step Pre-Order Lifecycle

Every order follows an automated 4-stage pipeline that guarantees zero waiting line congestion:

Stage What Happens Student Screen Kitchen KDS Screen
1. Placed Order placed from wallet. Database triggers instant WebSocket alert. Shows 4-digit token (#4826) and estimated prep time. Yellow ticket appears on KDS. Audio chime plays.
2. Preparing Kitchen staff taps "Start Cooking" and begins meal assembly. Live status bar advances to "Preparing". Blue ticket with live elapsed cooking timer.
3. Ready Staff packs meal and taps "Mark Ready". System sends push & SMS. Phone rings: "Order Ready for Pickup at Counter!" Emerald green badge in the pickup queue.
4. Collected Student shows token at counter. Staff hands hot meal and marks done. Order completed. Rating prompt appears. Order archived to daily sales report.

4. Wallet-First vs Traditional Payment

💡 Why V FOODS Uses a Wallet-First Model

Traditional food ordering apps run credit cards or UPI during checkout. When 400 students try to pay simultaneously during break, bank servers fail. By topping up a campus wallet in advance, checkout completes in under 20 milliseconds with zero payment dropouts.
